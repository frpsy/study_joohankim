If you pull back enough layers,
if you move beyond the noise,
you will quickly realize
that there’s nothing separating any of us
from one another.
That we’re all connected.
I have spent far too long trying to give this
a name.
To pin it down.
To understand it.
All I know is that I can feel it in the presence
of something beautiful.
I go there, to that place, when I create.
It feels like waking up from a deep slumber,
and seeing the universe with a child’s eyes.
When I go there, all of my fear and doubt
melts away.
And everything is energy.
It’s all just energy.
Limitless and immeasurable.
In our blindness, we tend to forget.
But it’s always there.
Raw and immense.
This is the place I go when I create -
Energy.
