You know, I've often wondered what the
word happy means.
I think a lot of people do.
We seem to do an awful lot to be it, and
I for one can't seem to fully understand
what it is.
If happiness came in a box that you
could order off of Amazon, I'm pretty
sure most of us would buy it.
But, it's just not that easy to get.
I mean, you can't measure it with a
ruler,
or hold it like a cup of coffee,
or wear it like a pair of socks.
I think George Orwell was onto something
when he said, "Happiness can exist only
in acceptance."
I like it because I think it's true.
It's okay not to feel bubbly and joyous
all the time.
Failure can be learned from, and makes
victory that much sweeter.
Like having ice cream
after eating broccolini or something.
But then sometimes we get stuck to the
idea that things can get us there, to
that happy place.
If I crave a pizza right now, and go
have one,
will I be happy?
It's so easy to say yes, but I don't
think that's how it works.
Because as enjoyable as it is, pizza
satisfies hunger and not the stuff
beyond that. Stuff like feeling loved by
others and by yourself.
The truth is,
being happy probably means different
things to different people.
I can only speak from personal
experience and in my eyes, it isn't so
simple in this crazy world because the
answers aren't given. And that's okay
because they were never meant to be.
We're here to learn, aren't we?
In the meantime,
I'm still waiting for that Amazon
package to arrive.
